Rules and instructions

Eligibility

Participants must register in the correct age group based on age on 22 November 2026.

Registration deadline

Online registration closes on 17 November 2026. Incomplete applications will not be accepted.

Asana selection

Select any 3 compulsory asanas from the 5 listed for your age group. Enter 2 different own-choice asanas.

Performance

Documents
